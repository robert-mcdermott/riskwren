# Glossary

Shared vocabulary. Most of these words can be renamed by an adopting organization; the meanings stay the same.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Acceptance** | A dated statement by a named person with the authority to make it that a risk above appetite will be lived with, for a reason, until a date |
| **Action** | Something a named person must do by a date, whatever it came from: a treatment plan, an issue, an incident |
| **Appetite** | The highest residual rating the organization is content to live with in a category of risk |
| **Applicability** | Whether a requirement applies within a scope; an exclusion carries a reason |
| **Assessment (of a risk)** | A dated judgment of likelihood and impact by a named person, with a rationale. Never edited; only superseded |
| **Assessment (of a scope)** | A planned set of tests of a scope's controls against a framework, over a period |
| **Attestation** | A dated statement by a named person that something is true: a control still operates as described, a unit's records are complete |
| **Authority table** | The rule that says who may accept a risk at each rating, and for how long |
| **Baseline** | A published selection of requirements from a framework, used as a scope's starting point |
| **Cadence** | How often something is revisited, set by rating or criticality |
| **Campaign** | A request to a set of people to do one thing by a date, with tracking of who has |
| **Control** | Something the organization does to reduce a risk or meet a requirement, described once, with an owner |
| **Criticality** | How much the organization depends on a process, system, or third party |
| **Crosswalk** | The mapping from controls to the requirements they help satisfy |
| **Data class** | An ordered level of data sensitivity, from public to restricted |
| **Effectiveness** | What is known about whether a control works: its design and its operation, each rated or shown as not assessed |
| **Escalation** | Raising a risk to a higher unit, with a reason |
| **Event** | One entry in the append-only history: who changed what, when, from what, to what |
| **Evidence** | A file, link, note, or attestation that supports a control, a test, or a closure, with an owner and a date until which it holds |
| **Exception** | An approved, time-limited departure from a control, a requirement, or a policy, with compensating controls |
| **Framework** | Any source of requirements: a standard, a regulation, an agreement, an internal policy |
| **Impact** | How bad the consequence would be, judged on several dimensions; the overall impact is the highest rated |
| **Implementation** | How one control is done within one scope: its status, who is responsible, and how |
| **Indicator** | A measure watched against thresholds because it warns of a risk |
| **Inherent rating** | The rating of a risk if its controls were absent or did not work |
| **Inheritance** | A scope relying on a control that another scope provides |
| **Issue** | Something found to be wrong that must be put right: a failed test, a finding, a gap |
| **Leader** | A person accountable for a unit, who sees its risks and may accept them up to a limit |
| **Likelihood** | How probable the event is, on a scale with written definitions |
| **Manager** | A member of the risk and compliance team, who runs the program |
| **Orphaned** | A record whose owner's account has been disabled |
| **Owner** | The named person accountable for a record and for keeping it current |
| **Pack** | A data file of content: a program, a library, a framework, a questionnaire |
| **Process** | Something the organization does and depends on |
| **Program** | The organization's configuration of RiskWren: taxonomy, scoring method, appetite, cadences, vocabularies |
| **Rating** | Low to Critical: how much is at stake, from likelihood and impact through the matrix |
| **Readiness** | For a scope and a framework, the state of each requirement, worked out from controls, tests, and evidence |
| **Reference** | A record's permanent, citable key, such as `RSK-0142` |
| **Reliance** | Whether a risk depends on a control (primary) or is merely helped by it (supporting) |
| **Requirement** | One citable statement within a framework, with a stable code |
| **Residual rating** | The rating of a risk given its controls as they actually are |
| **Response** | What is to be done about a risk: reduce, accept, transfer, or avoid |
| **Risk** | Something that could happen and would matter, stated as a cause, an event, and a consequence |
| **Scope** | A named boundary of units, processes, systems, and information that must meet one or more frameworks |
| **Scoring method** | The versioned document of scales, definitions, matrix, and weights by which risks are rated |
| **Standing** | Whether a risk is in order: within appetite, being treated, accepted, or needing a decision |
| **Steward** | A person who keeps a unit's records complete and current |
| **Suggested residual** | The residual rating the arithmetic proposes from what is known about a risk's controls, with its working |
| **System** | An application, platform, instrument, device, or piece of infrastructure |
| **Target rating** | Where a risk should be once its treatment is complete |
| **Test** | An examination of one control in one scope, with a procedure, a result, and a reviewer |
| **Third party** | An outside organization the organization depends on or shares with: a supplier, collaborator, sponsor, or subrecipient |
| **Treatment plan** | The actions that will bring a risk to its target rating, with an owner and a date |
| **Unit** | A node in the organization's hierarchy: a division, department, lab, clinic, or office |
